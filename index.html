Projects

Selected work

GradeRAG

In preparation · FIRE 2026

A retrieval-augmented QA system built around a persistent Mistake Knowledge Graph that lets the system learn from its own past failures across questions, not just within a single query. Evaluated across four LLMs and two QA benchmarks with McNemar's-test statistical validation.

  • RAG
  • FAISS
  • LLM Evaluation

Better Call MoLE

Under review · NLLP @ EMNLP 2026

Hierarchical Mixture of Legal Experts for Indian legal reasoning and question answering.

  • MoE
  • Legal NLP
  • QA

TalkingToM

Under review · SPELLL 2026

Dual-process cognitive modeling for narrative Theory of Mind question answering.

  • Theory of Mind
  • Reasoning

tensorveeras @ CC-MMD 2026

2nd place · ACM ICMI 2026

Cross-cultural misogynistic meme detection using a multimodal Mixture-of-Experts model fusing text and image encoders across culture-specific heads.

  • Multimodal
  • MoE
  • Content Moderation

ViBrainNet
